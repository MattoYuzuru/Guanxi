## Cordis decision

**Recommended: accept** upstream `cordis` **4.0.0-rc.11** and `@cordisjs/plugin-loader`
**1.0.0-rc.8**, exact pins, as npm dependencies behind `@guanxi/kernel`. DSH's hardened copy
(rc.7 + 24 changes) was read, not copied.

**Owner decision required before merge.** ADR-0015 validation 5 rejects Cordis on "a lifecycle
race found in review that upstream does not fix". Taken literally, it is met: upstream rc.11
leaks registrations made on unloading, never-activated or failed fibers, and the fixes are open,
unmerged PRs (cordiverse/cordis #96, #185, #91; async update races #194; loader rebind #218). The
architecture review's verdict was "accept with conditions", one of which is an owner amendment
of that criterion. The lane's evidence for accepting:

- every known race is contained in the facade and pinned by tests: one lifecycle guard plus four
  other workarounds, **5 of the 10-patch budget**, listed in
  [`packages/kernel/README.md`](../../../packages/kernel/README.md);
- `test/upstream-canary.test.ts` fails when an upgrade fixes the upstream defects, so the guard
  can be retired;
- both budgets are met with a wide margin (below).

Proposed amendment (for the owner, not applied here): criterion 3 becomes "a lifecycle race that
can be neither fixed upstream nor contained in the facade within the patch budget"; patches may
be runtime wrappers of the pinned package when each is counted and pinned by a test; the heap
gate bounds the object heap and reports total heap (see exit gate 2). If the owner instead keeps
the literal criterion, the fallback is ADR-0015's own kernel (1–2k lines); the facade surface in
`packages/kernel/src/index.ts` is what it would have to provide.

ADR-0022 is still `proposed`; the kernel implements its manifest, trust tiers and slots
provisionally.

## Residual risk

- The literal ADR-0015 criterion 3 is met; merge depends on the owner's amendment.
- The facade still exports Cordis's `Context` and `Service` and exposes fibers; swapping the
  kernel means re-implementing that surface. Narrow it to a `PluginContext` interface (K2/P1).
- Loader side effects at construction (`CORDIS_SHARED` JSON, native addon probe); replace it with
  an own reconciler before T1 plugins come from a registry (P1).
- The legacy server's governor and Ollama rows are pinned: they cannot reload until their
  consumers are plugins (K2/K3).
- The Ollama adapter combines a long-lived lifetime signal with `AbortSignal.any` per request;
  measured leak-free on Node 26.9 only, while `engines` allows Node ≥ 22.5.
- The sidebar slot is hidden below the `md` breakpoint (as the thread history is); plugin entries
  have no mobile surface yet. Plugin-supplied `href` values are not validated; needed before
  T1 web plugins.
- `__GUANXI_KERNEL__` exists in development builds only.
