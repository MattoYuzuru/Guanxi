# Redesign research — 2026-10-23

**Status:** accepted direction for the UI-redesign lane (D-200–D-207); palette values pending
owner approval · **Updated:** 2026-10-23

The owner retired the verdigris look and asked for a white session surface, a bluer accent, a
plentiful second tone for the chrome, icons instead of prose, no retry buttons or visible
polling, one interface instead of Developer/Visual, custom controls, and an optional "max
graphics" tier ([D-200–D-210](../../decisions/2026-10-23-owner-decisions-addendum.md)). This
note records what we studied, what we adopt and what we reject. Sources were opened on
2026-10-23. Other products are references for interaction patterns only; Guanxi keeps its
own identity ([BRAND](../BRAND.md)).

## 2. Techniques

- **Layered shadows.** Several layers sharing one light angle, offsets doubling, hue-tinted
  rather than black; never animate them ([Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/css/designing-shadows/)).
- **Inner highlight.** `inset 0 1px 0` white at low alpha lifts a control; pressed states use
  an inset shadow ([inset shadows](https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/css-inset-box-shadow-guide/)).
- **Hairlines.** 1 px borders at about 3:1 where they are the only boundary of a control;
  decorative hairlines may be lighter ([1px borders](https://1px.com/1px-css-borders/)).
- **Grain.** Live `feTurbulence` shifts colours and costs per frame; a static noise tile at low
  opacity is the safe form ([Grainy gradients](https://css-tricks.com/grainy-gradients/)).
- **Type.** `font-optical-sizing: auto`; `text-wrap: balance` on headings only, `pretty` on
  prose ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/font-optical-sizing),
  [Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/css-ui/css-text-wrap-balance),
  [WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/16547/better-typography-with-text-wrap-pretty/)).
- **Springs.** `linear()` approximates a spring in pure CSS and is Baseline since 2023
  ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/easing-function/linear)).
- **Reduced motion and transparency.** Respect both; `prefers-reduced-transparency` is not
  Baseline, so opaque is the default and translucency the enhancement
  ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-transparency)).
- **Shader backgrounds.** Cap the device pixel ratio near 1.5 or render at half resolution,
  run near 30 fps, stop on `visibilitychange` and off screen, keep a static CSS fallback
  ([shader backgrounds](https://21st.dev/blog/react-shader-background-components)).
- **OKLCH palettes.** Perceptual lightness makes generated scales predictable; Radix's twelve
  steps give the role map Guanxi already uses
  ([Evil Martians](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/oklch-in-css-why-quit-rgb-hsl),
  [Radix scale](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale)).

## 3. What we adopt

1. **A white session pane floating on a tinted chrome.** The canvas (step 2) is the plentiful
   chrome tone; panes are step 1 (white in light, a deep neutral in dark), rounded, with a
   hairline and a two-layer shadow. The sidebar and status strip sit directly on the chrome.
2. **Generated palettes.** `packages/ui/tokens/palettes/generate.mjs` builds every candidate
   in OKLCH and solves lightness so control borders reach 3:1, muted text 4.5:1 and secondary
   text 7:1 against both the chrome and the pane.
3. **Icons with anchored popovers** in the status strip: device, memory, quota, keep-awake,
   power, runtime and inbox. Values carry the weight (`16.8 GB`), labels stay quiet.
4. **One interface.** No Developer/Visual switch; density, sidebar placement, graphics tier
   and motion live in a borderless customisation popover in the chrome.
5. **The composer pattern** of D-207: centred when the session is empty, docked after the
   first prompt; permission mode at its lower left; model, effort and context at its lower
   right; Stop replaces Send while running.
6. **Live data without visible polling.** Push where the node streams, quiet background
   refresh elsewhere, structural sharing so unchanged data does not re-render, errors kept
   until a success replaces them, automatic recovery with backoff instead of Retry buttons.
7. **Short, interruptible motion** on `transform` and `opacity` with the existing motion
   tokens; nothing moves under reduced motion.
8. **Max graphics** as an opt-in tier: one WebGL canvas behind the chrome, half resolution,
   about 30 fps, paused when hidden or under reduced motion, with a static gradient fallback.

## 4. What we reject

1. Glass or gradients behind reading text: panes, transcript and code stay opaque.
2. Animated blur and animated multi-layer shadows (cost, Comeau).
3. Copying the Stripe or whatamesh shader code (licence unverifiable, ADR-0016); our shader is
   written from scratch.
4. A cream canvas: BRAND names cream with a warm accent as the most common generated look,
   and the Linen candidate is kept only for comparison.
5. Blue-violet gradients (BRAND boundary); the shader uses the accent and chrome hues only.
6. `transition: all`, keyframe-only interactions, slow enters on frequent actions.
7. Unicode or emoji glyphs as icons, including the theme toggle (D-205).
8. Decorative middle dots between phrases (D-202) and "updated N s ago" captions (D-203).

## 5. Palette candidates

All three keep the session surface white in light and a deep neutral in dark, and differ in the
chrome tone and the accent hue. Ratios are WCAG 2.x, measured against the worse of chrome and
pane. Generated values: `packages/ui/tokens/palettes/candidates.json`; the catalog preview is
the `palette-preview` component (Settings → Developer → Components).

| Candidate | Light chrome / pane / accent | Dark chrome / pane / accent | Text, muted, accent (light) | Text, muted, accent (dark) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Porcelain + Cobalt** (working default) | `#EFF3F8` / `#FFFFFF` / `#285DD2` | `#0D1014` / `#16191D` / `#739DEF` | 15.85, 4.74, 5.25 | 15.41, 4.75, 7.08 |
| Mist + Indigo | `#F1F2F9` / `#FFFFFF` / `#4D5AC8` | `#0F0F15` / `#18181E` / `#8899EA` | 15.83, 4.74, 5.21 | 15.48, 4.70, 7.09 |
| Linen + Azure | `#F5F2ED` / `#FFFFFF` / `#0064C1` | `#110F0D` / `#1B1816` / `#63A1E5` | 15.84, 4.75, 5.23 | 15.44, 4.75, 7.07 |

**Working default: Porcelain + Cobalt, pending owner approval.** It is the bluest accent the
owner asked for, keeps the chrome neutral rather than cream (BRAND) and stays clear of
blue-violet (BRAND). Mist + Indigo drifts toward violet; Linen + Azure is warm and attractive
but reads as the cream look BRAND warns against. Switching to another candidate is a token
change: `node packages/ui/tokens/palettes/apply.mjs <id>` rewrites the primitive colours, the
FOUNDATIONS tables are regenerated from the same values, and `npm run tokens:build` emits CSS.
